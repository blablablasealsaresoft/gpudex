Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 27, 2025
Last Updated: January 27, 2025

1. Introduction

GPUDx ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our decentralized GPU rental platform.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Wallet addresses and transaction data
  • Account preferences and settings
  • Communications with our support team
  • KYC information for enterprise accounts (if required)

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage patterns and platform interactions
  • Device information and browser type
  • IP addresses and location data
  • Performance metrics and error logs

2.3 Blockchain Data

As a blockchain-based platform, certain transactions and interactions are permanently recorded on public blockchains and cannot be deleted.

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Service Provision

  • Facilitating GPU rentals and payments
  • Managing staking and reward distributions
  • Providing customer support
  • Maintaining platform security

3.2 Platform Improvement

  • Analyzing usage patterns to improve services
  • Developing new features and capabilities
  • Optimizing platform performance

3.3 Legal Compliance

  • Complying with applicable laws and regulations
  • Preventing fraud and abuse
  • Responding to legal requests

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 We Do Not Sell Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4.2 Limited Sharing

We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • With GPU providers to facilitate rentals
  • With service providers who assist our operations
  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect rights, property, or safety
  • In connection with business transfers

4.3 Anonymized Data

We may share aggregated, anonymized data that cannot identify individual users for research and analytics purposes.

5. Data Security

5.1 Security Measures

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Multi-factor authentication requirements
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Access controls and monitoring systems

5.2 Incident Response

In the event of a security incident, we will notify affected users and authorities as required by law.

5.3 User Responsibility

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their private keys and wallet credentials.

6. Data Retention

6.1 Retention Periods

  • Account data: Retained while account is active plus 7 years
  • Transaction data: Retained for 7 years for compliance
  • Usage logs: Retained for 2 years for optimization
  • Blockchain data: Permanently stored on public blockchains

6.2 Data Deletion

Upon account closure, we will delete or anonymize personal data according to our retention schedule, except where retention is required by law.

7. Your Rights and Choices

7.1 Access and Portability

You have the right to access and export your personal data in a machine-readable format.

7.2 Correction and Updates

You can update your account information and preferences through your dashboard.

7.3 Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.

7.4 Marketing Communications

You can opt out of marketing communications at any time through your account settings or unsubscribe links.

8. International Data Transfers

As a global platform, your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We implement appropriate safeguards to protect your data during international transfers.

9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Cookies and Tracking

10.1 Cookie Usage

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Maintain user sessions and preferences
  • Analyze platform usage and performance
  • Provide personalized experiences

10.2 Cookie Controls

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may limit platform functionality.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Users will be notified of material changes through the platform or email. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
